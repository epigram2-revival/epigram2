Epigram 2 — Revival

Epigram 2 is the second-generation dependently typed programming language and interactive programming environment by Conor McBride, James McKinna, Peter Morris and colleagues (St Andrews / Durham / Nottingham, ~2004–2010) — the project behind Why Dependent Types Matter (2005). Its ideas — elaboration as an interactive activity, programming by refining holes, ornaments — directly shaped today's ecosystem: Idris is the explicit "Epigram with user-friendly features" successor, and Agda's interactive elaboration owes it a visible debt.

This repository is a revival fork: the goal is a living Epigram 2 — buildable on modern toolchains, documented, its 74 open issues triaged, and its ideas accessible to a new generation of dependently typed programmers.

Status (2026-08-30): port in progress, built in the open. The preprocessing pipeline is green (GHC 9.10.3); the elaborator-syntax policy decision (~767 sites) is the open item. The golden-harness hardening and the CI gate protocol are in the tree (CI_GATE_SPEC.md); CI runs are pending a workflow-scoped token. Nothing here claims a green build until the harness says so — that is the point of the gate.

Build instructions (short)

Requires a modern GHC (9.2–9.10) with cabal. The tree is expected red until the elaborator-syntax port lands — see CI_GATE_SPEC.md for the receipt discipline around any green claim.

cabal build all
cd test && ./test.sh
